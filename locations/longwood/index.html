Frequently Asked Questions — Longwood Concrete Driveways

What does a concrete driveway cost in Longwood, FL?

Longwood concrete driveways cost $5-$11 per square foot depending on style and size. Homes near Big Tree Park or the Wekiva area can request free on-site estimates.

Do you serve the Wekiva Springs area near Longwood?

Yes, we serve all of Longwood including Wekiva Springs, Sabal Point, Sweetwater area, and neighborhoods along SR-434 and CR-427.

Is concrete a good choice for Longwood's sandy soil?

Yes, with proper base preparation. We compact and add road base material over Longwood's sandy soil to create a stable foundation that prevents settling and cracking.

Can you install a circular driveway in Longwood?

Absolutely. Circular and curved driveways are popular on Longwood's larger lots. We handle the extra forming and grading work these designs require.
